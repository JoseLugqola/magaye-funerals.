Our Story

Who We Are

Magaye Funerals was built on a single belief — that every life deserves a dignified farewell, regardless of circumstance.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, compassionate, and high-quality funeral services to all South African families through affordable subscription plans.

Our Vision

Our Promise

Every family that trusts us will experience seamless, professional support from the moment of loss to the final farewell.

The Beginning

A Legacy Built on Compassion

Magaye Funerals was founded with one goal in mind: to remove the financial and logistical burden from grieving families at the most difficult time of their lives.

"We started this business because we saw families struggle. Grief is hard enough — the paperwork, the logistics, the costs — should never be."

Today, Magaye Funerals serves over 1,200 active subscribers across three comprehensive plan tiers, providing everything from collection and storage to full burial logistics. We are fully licensed, community-focused, and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
