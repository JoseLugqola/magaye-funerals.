# User Manual & Operations Guide
## Magaye Funerals Platform

Welcome to the Magaye Funerals Digital Management Platform. This guide explains how to navigate and use the platform depending on your role.

---

## 1. Accessing the Platform
- **Public Portal:** Navigate to `guest.html`. Here you can browse services, view the 'About Us' and 'Contact' pages, and initiate self-registration.
- **Customer Portal:** Navigate to `customer_login.html`.
- **Staff Portal:** Navigate to `staff_login.html`.

---

## 2. Guide for Customers (Policyholders)

### 2.1 Registering for a New Plan
1. From the public homepage (`guest.html`), click **Join a Plan**.
2. **Step 1:** Enter your personal details (Name, ID, Phone) and upload a copy of your ID.
3. **Step 2:** Select a coverage tier (Basic, Standard, Heritage) and who you want to cover (Myself, Spouse, +5 Dependants).
4. **Step 3:** Add the details of your dependants (if applicable).
5. **Step 4:** Review the summary, create an account password, agree to the Terms of Service, and click **Activate My Policy**.

### 2.2 Logging In
1. Go to `customer_login.html`.
2. Enter the 13-digit SA ID number you used to register.
3. Enter the password you created during registration.
4. Click **Access Dashboard**.

### 2.3 Using Your Member Dashboard
Once logged in to your dashboard (`customer_dashboard.html`), you can:
- **View Policy Status:** See your active plan, monthly premium, and next payment date.
- **Download Certificate:** Click the **Policy Document** link in the side menu to view and print your official Policy Activation Certificate.
- **Add Dependants:** In the "Your Dependants" card, click **Add Dependant**, fill in their details, and upload their ID document.
- **Report a Claim:** Click the **Submit Digital Claim** button in the red sidebar card. Enter the deceased's name, date of incident, and upload the death certificate to notify the parlor immediately.
- **Compare Plans:** If you wish to upgrade, click the **Compare Plans** tab to see other available tiers.

## 3. Guide for Staff (Administrators)

### 3.1 Logging In
1. Go to `staff_login.html`.
2. Enter your authorized staff email address and password.
3. Click **Enter Portal**.

### 3.2 Using the Executive Dashboard (`index.html`)
- Upon login, you will see the **Dashboard Overview**.
- This page displays key metrics: Total Active Members, Monthly Revenue collected, Pending Claims requiring attention, and Recent Registration/Payment activity.
- The sidebar provides quick navigation to all administrative modules. On mobile devices, click the hamburger menu icon (bottom right) to open the sidebar.

### 3.3 Registering a Walk-in Member (`registration.html`)
If a client visits the parlor in person:
1. Navigate to **New Registration** on the sidebar.
2. Fill out the multi-step digital form on their behalf.
3. Add their dependants and assign them a plan.
4. The system will automatically generate their policy and add them to the member directory.

### 3.4 Managing Members (`directory.html`)
1. Navigate to **Member Directory**.
2. Here you can view a list of all clients.
3. You can use the **Search** bar to find a member by name or ID.
4. You can filter the list to only show "Active", "Waiting", or "Lapsed" policies.
5. Click **View Profile** to see a member's full details and their dependants.

### 3.5 Processing Payments (`payments.html`)
1. Navigate to **Payments**.
2. Click **Record New Payment**.
3. Enter the Member ID. The system will automatically fetch their name and required monthly premium.
4. Select the payment method (Cash, EFT, Debit Order) and enter the amount paid.
5. Click **Process Payment**. This will log the transaction and update the member's account status.
6. A digital receipt will be generated.

### 3.6 Managing Claims (`claims.html`)
1. Navigate to **Claims**.
2. You will see a list of all claims submitted by policyholders or captured by agents.
3. Click **Review** on a "Pending" claim to view the uploaded Death Certificate and deceased details.
4. Verify the documents and the member's waiting period status.
5. Update the status to **Approved** (to initiate funeral arrangements) or **Rejected** (if invalid).
