Life Insurance Policy Document

Official Terms & Conditions

1. Introduction

This policy outlines the terms and conditions governing the life insurance cover provided to the Policyholder (hereafter referred to as “the Main Member”). The policy is subject to the payment of premiums and adherence to all terms stated herein.

2. Definitions

  • Main Member: The individual in whose name the policy is issued and who is covered under this policy.
  • Insurer: Magaye Funerals (PTY) LTD, the company providing the insurance cover.
  • Policy Commencement Date: The date on which the policy becomes active after acceptance and receipt of the first premium.
  • Waiting Period: A specified period during which certain benefits are limited or excluded.
  • Accidental Death: Death caused solely and directly by violent, external, and unforeseen events.

3. Eligibility and Cover Limitations

  • This policy is limited to one Main Member only.
  • No additional members, dependents, or beneficiaries are covered unless explicitly stated in a separate endorsement.
  • The Main Member must meet all underwriting and eligibility requirements set by the Insurer.

4. Premium Payment

  • Cover under this policy will commence only after the first premium has been received and accepted by the Insurer.
  • Premiums must be paid in accordance with the agreed frequency (monthly, quarterly, or annually).
  • Failure to pay premiums may result in lapse or termination of the policy.

5. Waiting Period for Natural Death

A six (6) month waiting period applies from the Policy Commencement Date for death due to natural causes.

If the Main Member dies due to natural causes within this waiting period:

  • The Insurer will not pay the full insured benefit.
  • Instead, the Insurer may refund premiums paid, with or without interest, depending on policy terms.

After the waiting period has expired, full cover for natural death will apply.

6. Accidental Death Benefit

  • Cover for accidental death is effective immediately from the Policy Commencement Date, provided the first premium has been paid.
  • In the event of accidental death, the full insured benefit will be payable, regardless of the waiting period for natural death.
  • The Insurer reserves the right to request supporting documentation to verify the cause of death.

7. Exclusions

The policy will not pay benefits under the following circumstances (including but not limited to):

  • Suicide within the first 12 months from the Policy Commencement Date.
  • Death resulting from unlawful or criminal activities.
  • Misrepresentation or non-disclosure of material information during application.
  • Participation in hazardous activities unless disclosed and accepted by the Insurer.

8. Claims Process

All claims must be submitted in writing within a reasonable period following the death of the Main Member. Required documentation may include:

  • Certified copy of death certificate
  • Identity documents
  • Medical or police reports (in case of accidental death)

9. Policy Termination

This policy will terminate upon:

  • Payment of the insured benefit
  • Non-payment of premiums beyond the grace period
  • Cancellation by the Policyholder
  • Fraud or material misrepresentation

10. General Conditions

  • The policy constitutes the entire agreement between the Insurer and the Policyholder.
  • Any amendments must be made in writing and approved by the Insurer.
  • This policy is governed by the applicable laws and regulations of South Africa.

11. Beneficiary Nomination

  • The Main Member may nominate one or more beneficiaries to receive the policy benefits.
  • In the absence of a valid nomination, benefits will be paid to the estate of the deceased.

12. Grace Period

  • A grace period (typically 15–31 days) will be allowed for late premium payments.
  • During the grace period, cover remains in force.
