1. Policy Coverage

By subscribing to Magaye Funerals, you agree to the terms of your selected plan (Basic, Standard, or Heritage). Coverage is subject to the following conditions:

  • Coverage applies to the Principal Member and registered dependants only.
  • Accuracy of information (ID numbers, names) is the responsibility of the subscriber.
  • Benefits are provided as services or specified cash vouchers, as outlined in the Plan Comparison.

2. Waiting Periods

To ensure sustainability and fairness, the following waiting periods apply:

  • Natural Death: 6-month waiting period from the date of the first successful premium payment.
  • Accidental Death: Immediate cover after the first successful premium payment.
  • Suicide: 12-month waiting period applies.

3. Premium Payments

  • Premiums are due on the 1st or 7th of every month.
  • A grace period of 15 days is allowed.
  • Policies with two consecutive missed payments will be automatically Frozen.
  • Frozen policies require a reactivation fee and may be subject to a new waiting period.

4. Claims Process

Claims must be reported within 48 hours of the event. Required documentation includes:

  • Certified copy of the Death Certificate.
  • Certified copy of the Deceased's ID.
  • Certified copy of the Claimant's ID.
  • BI-1663 form (Notification of Death).

5. Cancellation

Members may cancel their policy at any time with 30 days' notice. Please note that premiums paid are non-refundable as they represent the cost of risk cover provided during the active period.
