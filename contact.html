Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers to the questions families most commonly ask about our plans and services.

How do I register for a plan?

You can register online through our registration page in under 5 minutes, or contact an agent who will assist you.

What is the waiting period?

Policies include a standard waiting period for natural death claims. Accidental death is covered immediately from the first day of your policy being active and paid up.

Can I include my children?

Yes. The "Principal + 5" cover type includes your spouse and up to 5 dependants. Children under a specified age are covered at no additional premium.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept EFT, debit order, cash payments at our offices, and mobile money transfers. Monthly premiums are due by the 5th of each month.

What happens if I miss a payment?

We will contact you immediately. Policies may be suspended after one missed payment , and lapsed after two consecutive months without payment.

How do I file a claim?

Call our 24/7 emergency line immediately after a bereavement. Our team will guide you through the process and begin collecting your loved one at the earliest opportunity.
